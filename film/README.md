# Không — a 64-second film

Painted as a light ink-wash day: paper sky, vermilion sun, misty blue-green hills, a torii gate, drifting sakura petals.

An empty brush circle (ensō, the Zen symbol of emptiness) is the only character. It has no face: it shows attention with the gap in its stroke and feeling with squash, stretch and breath.

1. A drop of ink falls; the circle draws itself and wakes up.
2. Voices in many languages ripple in; it turns toward each one and writes down what it hears.
3. One mind becomes many — small circles fly out to stones of knowledge, bring back light, and a graph forms.
4. A rough boulder lands; the circle chisels it into a smooth pebble, and everything moves faster (5 s → 2 s).
5. Agents pass through a stone gate — one that isn't sure yet is held back. Then it climbs a hill, one step at a time.
6. Lanterns light up across the valley; a few paper boats drift down the river.
7. Mist. The small circle rises and becomes the great ensō — `model.fit(curiosity, epochs=∞)`.
